# Movida — Launch video & social media strategy

> Positioning: **"What's your next dance event?"** — international congresses, festivals and the socials around them, seen through your crew.
> Voice: playful, warm, community-first. Primary persona: **the travelling social dancer** (goes to 3–10 congresses/festivals a year, dances locally every week). Secondary: **organisers & teachers**.

---

## Part 1 — Hero video (30–40 s, vertical 9:16)

**Goal:** stop the scroll, show the "aha" (everything in one place + friends going + passport), drive a tap to joinmovida.com.
**Platforms:** Instagram Reels (primary), TikTok, YouTube Shorts. Also cut a 15 s version for Stories and ads.

### Script & shot list

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voice / audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Close-up: dancer's thumb frantically swiping IG stories, WhatsApp groups, a crumpled flyer. Quick cuts. | **"Where are we dancing next?"** | Trending upbeat salsa/bachata audio (licensed in-app). Optional VO: "Okay… where's the party?" |
| 2–5 s | Phone shows 14 unread group chats "is anyone going??" | "14 group chats. 0 answers." | — |
| 5–7 s | Dancer sighs → opens Movida from the home screen (app icon visible) | "…or just open Movida" | Beat drop |

| 12–17 s | Tap the event → "3 friends going" avatars → tap **I'm going** | "See who's going before you go 👀" | — |
| 17–23 s | Program grid → pick 3 workshops into **My Plan** | "Build your festival plan in seconds" | — |
| 23–29 s | Cut to real footage: the dancer at the festival with friends, dancing (real social floor shot) | — | Music swells |
| 29–34 s | Dance Passport badge unlock animation: "City Hopper 🏙️" + countries counter | "Every social, stamped." | — |
| 34–40 s | Logo + URL + "Add to Home Screen" gesture | **joinmovida.com — What's your next dance event?** | Last beat |

### Production notes (phone-shot, ~0 budget)
- Record screens on a real phone with seeded demo data (use the `prod-showcase` scenario so no real users appear). Hide the status bar clock/battery or set 9:41.
- Real-life shots: 2–3 dancers you know, at a real social or festival (ask organiser + people on camera for consent, in writing).
- Captions burned in (85 % watch muted). Large font, high contrast, inside the 9:16 safe zone.
- Use in-app licensed music only (Reels/TikTok libraries). Never upload copyrighted tracks with the file.
- Export 1080×1920, < 60 s. Cover frame = the map full of pins + "Where are we dancing next?".

### Variants (A/B test hooks)
1. **Traveller cut** — hook: "Landing in Lisbon on Friday. Where do we dance?" → map of Lisbon → socials tonight → friends going.
2. **Group-chat cut** — hook: "POV: 'is anyone going?' for the 40th time" → I'm going + friends going.
3. **Passport cut** — hook: "How many countries have you danced in?" → passport stats + badges → "Start yours."
4. **Organiser cut (30 s)** — hook: "Organisers: your Instagram reach is 4 %." → "Update your Google Calendar once → it's on the Movida map" → program change notifies everyone → verified badge → "List your events free".

### 60–90 s explainer (YouTube, landing page, pitch to partners)
1. The problem (scattered events) — 10 s
2. Discover: For You, map, filters — 15 s
3. Plan with friends: going, program, reminders, tickets — 20 s
4. Track: passport, memories, reviews — 15 s
5. For organisers: auto-import, claims, program updates — 15 s
6. CTA — 5 s

## Part 2 — Social media strategy (first 3 months)

### Goals & KPIs
| Goal | KPI | Target month 1 → 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach, video views, profile visits | 50k → 250k views |
| Acquisition | Visits to joinmovida.com (UTM), installs, sign-ups | 500 → 3,000 sign-ups |
| Activation | % sign-ups who tap "I'm going" in week 1 | ≥ 40 % |
| Engagement | Weekly active users, passport shares | WAU 30 % of sign-ups |
| Supply | Organisers claiming events / sharing calendars | 10 → 50 |

Track with UTM links (`?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reel&utm_campaign=launch_hero`) and Umami (consented visitors) + sign-up counts in Admin.

### Channels
| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** (main) | Reels, carousels, stories, collabs with dancers/festivals | 3–4 posts/week + daily stories |
| **TikTok** | Reels re-cut natively, trends, humour | 3 posts/week |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Re-post Reels; explainer on channel | 2–3/week |
| **Facebook dance groups** | Weekly "this weekend" posts where allowed by admins | 1/week per group |
| **WhatsApp / Telegram communities** | Share passport/event links (rich previews) via ambassadors | Organic |

### Content pillars
1. **"What's next?" guides (30 %)** — "Festivals this month in Europe", "Where to dance in Berlin this weekend". Carousels made from Movida data; last slide = "Full map on Movida".
2. **Dance-life humour (20 %)** — relatable POVs: group chats, packing for a congress, "one more song".
3. **Passport stories (15 %)** — dancers sharing their passport/badges; UGC repost with permission. Monthly "Passport of the month".
4. **Organiser spotlights (15 %)** — 30 s features of a festival/social; co-post as Collab.
5. **Feature tips (10 %)** — "3 things you didn't know Movida does".
6. **Behind the scenes (10 %)** — the dancer who built Movida, roadmap, asking the community what to build next.

### Launch phases
**Phase 0 — Pre-launch (2–3 weeks)**
- Fill the map: make sure the next 3 months of major European congresses/festivals and socials in 3–5 hub cities are listed.
- 20–30 beta dancers (your network) → collect quotes, passport screenshots, bug feedback.
- Teaser stories: "Something for dancers who travel… 👀".

**Phase 1 — Launch week (anchored on a festival partner, e.g. Back 2 Mambo)**
- Publish the hero video + traveller cut.
- Festival program available in Movida with My Plan → partner announces it to their attendees ("Plan your workshops on Movida").
- Ambassadors share their invite links; QR code on a flyer/table card at the festival.
- Daily stories from the festival floor.

**Phase 2 — Growth (weeks 2–12)**
- Weekly "What's next" carousel + 2 humour Reels + 1 spotlight.
- Ambassador program: 1 ambassador per city/scene, personal invite link, monthly shout-out, perks from partner organisers (guest-list spots, promo codes).
- Organiser outreach: DM 10 organisers/week with "your events are already on Movida — claim them".
- Monthly challenge: "Stamp a new city this month" → share your passport.

### Paid tests (small budget)
- Boost the best-performing organic Reel only (never cold ads).
- Meta (IG) + TikTok, €5–10/day per ad set, 3–5 days, audiences: interests salsa/bachata/kizomba/zouk + "travel", 18–45, EU.
- Kill anything with CPC > €0.50 or install-to-click < 15 % after 3 days; double the winner.
- Retarget site visitors (only with consent-based tracking; no pixel without consent).

### Community management
- Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting.
- Feature user content only with explicit permission (DM "Can we repost?" + credit).
- Never repost event photos/flyers without the organiser's OK.
- Escalate privacy/report requests to support@joinmovida.com.
