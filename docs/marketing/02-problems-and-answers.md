# Movida — Problems we solve (positioning & messaging)

> Voice: **playful, warm, community-first**. We talk like a dance friend, not a SaaS. Short sentences, a wink, emojis in moderation (💃🕺🔥🌍). Never mock other platforms by name; mock the *situation*.

Core promise: **"What's your next dance event?" — Movida answers in one place, with your crew.**

---

## 1. Search — "Where's the party?"

| Pain today | Movida's answer | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Events are scattered across Instagram stories (gone in 24h), WhatsApp/Telegram groups, Facebook events, flyers, websites | One map + calendar that pulls organisers' public calendars automatically | "Stop doom-scrolling 12 stories to find tonight's social." |
| Hard to filter: is it salsa or bachata? Social or class? Free? | Style, event type, area and date filters | "Bachata Thursday? Kizomba Sunday? Filter, done." |
| Travelling to a new city / planning a festival trip — you don't know the scene | International events + city-level map, saved searches with alerts | "Landing in Lisbon? Know where to dance before the plane lands." |
| Duplicate, outdated, cancelled info | Deduplication, organiser edits and cancellation notices | "If it's cancelled, you'll know. Before you put your shoes on." |
| Algorithms decide what you see | Your own feed: your styles, your area, your friends | "Your feed, your styles. No algorithm DJ." |

## 2. Plan — "Who's going? Which workshops?"

| Pain today | Movida's answer | Message |
|---|---|---|
| "Are you going?" ×20 in group chats | "I'm going" with friends visible on every event | "See who's going before you go." |
| Festival programs are a 3-room poster puzzle | Program grid + My Plan, with change alerts | "Build your festival plan in two minutes. We'll ping you if a workshop moves." |
| Tickets lost in email, screenshots everywhere | Private ticket wallet on the event | "Your ticket lives on the event. Not in your inbox abyss." |
| Questions to the organiser get lost | Event Q&A, rides, accommodation threads | "Need a ride to the congress? Ask the room." |
| Forgetting | Reminders by push/email, calendar export | "We remember so you can focus on your shines." |

## 5. Organisers & community actors

| Pain today | Movida's answer | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram reach keeps dropping; posts reach only existing followers | Listed on a map dancers actually browse; followers notified of new events | "Reach dancers who don't follow you — yet." |
| Copy-pasting events to 5 platforms | Public Google Calendar imported automatically | "Update your calendar once. Movida does the rest." |
| Last-minute changes don't reach people | Edits and cancellations pushed to "going" + My Plan users | "Room change at 11pm? Everyone who planned it knows." |
| No structured feedback | Reviews with mood + aspects | "Know what made the night great." |
| Credibility | Verified organiser badge, hosting page | "Your events, your badge, your page." |
| Teachers / curators want to share picks | Curator profiles people follow | "Be the friend who always knows where to go." |

## 6. Why Movida (differentiators)
- **Built for social dancers**, not generic events.
- **Free, no ads, no selling data.**
- **Installable without an app store** — one link, works on any phone.
- **Privacy you control**, per event.
- **Events arrive automatically** from organisers' calendars.
- **Fun**: passport, badges, streaks.
